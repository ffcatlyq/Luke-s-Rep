---

The Roche logo is a unique and iconic symbol of the company. The modernised Roche logo provides optimal impact and legibility across all media – digital, print and other.

The logo consists of the Roche wordmark set in the Roche Sans typeface and contained within the recognisable hexagon-shaped outline. The primary colour for the Roche logo is Roche Blue.

[Visit the logo download section here.](https://branding.roche.com/document/53)

---

The Roche logo can be used in three colours:

- **Roche Blue: **The main colour version of the logo to use whenever possible
- **White:** To use for enhanced visibility (if the background provides enough contrast)
- **Black:** To use only where Roche Blue cannot be used due to printing restrictions
To bring out the warmth in the Roche brand, in addition to white, it is recommended to use the Roche blue logo on top of the four core Roche [base tones](https://branding.roche.com/document/26#/colours/base-colours) as shown in the image below.

---

The Blue or White logo can also sit over an image, providing there is enough contrast for legibility, as shown in the examples below.

---

For digital applications the logo should sit top left and for print applications the logo sits top right.

There are exceptions to this rule for certain applications (e.g. signage pylons) where this cannot be adhered to. Please consult the Corporate Brand Team when these instances arise.

---

The appearance of the Roche logo must remain consistent. The Roche logo should not be modified. No attempt should be made to alter the Roche logo in any way.
> **[warning]** The hexagon shape is used exclusively in the logo and must not be used for any other purpose.
