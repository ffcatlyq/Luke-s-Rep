Colour is integral to the Roche brand language. The vibrant, warm blue of the Roche logo combined with a selection of warm natural tones, inspired by nature, create a consistent meaningful brand with an emotional appearance.
_(video: Roche_Brand_Portal_Animation_Colour_Palette.mp4)_

Primary and Accent tones provide pops of colour and focus where necessary. They are used in combination with large white space and neutral base colours, such as grey. Base colours play a key role in the design by providing the backdrop to bring warmth to the visual expression.
