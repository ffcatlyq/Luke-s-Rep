The unique type families for Roche were designed with a nod to Roche’s design history, the requirements of the present, and to be lasting well into the future.

The Roche Sans, Condensed, and Serif typefaces are a core part of the new brand identity and must be used on all Roche touchpoints. The fonts cover 90+ languages using Latin and Cyrillic alphabets.
_(video: Roche_Brand_Portal_Animation_Typography.mp4)_

Carefully crafted and optimised, with a strong focus on digital and maximum readability across various screen sizes, the result of using the typography should feel simple, elegant and minimalistic.

---

The new Roche font family consists of three font styles: Roche Sans, Roche Sans Condensed and Roche Serif. Each style comes in a variety of weights as seen in the picture below.

Depending on where the fonts are used, their sizes vary. 

All available templates have been preconfigured to use optimum font sizes. For more detailed information about typography, please consult the section “for professional designers”.
Generally speaking, all text should be set in black, dark greys or white (on dark backgrounds). Roche Blue can be used sparingly to highlight individual words. For more colour usage definitions, please go to the [corresponding page](/r/0bfqSa_4JEm0vxI_RmT8hT2Cd5ug2zdEqGqQopg6A3E).

As a rule of thumb, accent colours should not be used in typography, with the exception of wordmarks. To create a wordmark, the Roche [wordmark generator](/r/CglvpJ836uC88Wovp9DqNi6lsNOEvQOXtfEBAQIvFKU) should be used.

As your first font choice for languages not supported by Roche Sans and Roche Serif (such as Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Vietnamese or Arabic - amon others), we recommend using [Google Noto Sans](https://fonts.google.com/noto/fonts) in its different variations, as Google Noto covers a wide range of languages.

Where the use of Google fonts is not practicable, alternative fonts can be identified in collaboration with the Corporate Brand Team.

For all Roche channels and materials that fall under the Roche corporate brand, either the Roche font family or Google Noto have to be used (see above).

If you choose to utilise any other font (commercial or open-licence) for whatever reason, please be aware that you will be fully held accountable for any potential legal ramifications and penalties resulting from such use.
