Roche corporate policy is to **decline requests** from outside vendors and contractors to use the Roche name, logo, company information, and employee testimonials for vendor press releases, case studies, campaigns, roadshow presentations, websites, social media posts, sales brochures, or other promotional materials.

Roche declines these requests for use of our name primarily because of the potential that such use will be perceived as or implied to be an endorsement. In addition, the use of the Roche name for such purposes dilutes the corporate brand over time.

The policy is strict as any deviations are quickly copied from one area to the other, so these requests from the partner/vendor should be declined.

There are three exceptions to the rule:

- **Listing Roche as a client on a website:** vendors, contractors, business development collaborators, may use the Roche logo on their website under “clients”. The logo use is restricted to the approved purpose only and is limited to two years.
- **Listing Roche as a sponsor: **external organisations and events (e.g. conferences and symposia) – alone or in conjunction with other parties – may use the Roche logo on their website or other materials.The logo use is restricted to the approved purpose only and is limited to the duration of the sponsorship.
- **Listing Roche as a partner:** non-profit organisations, hospitals, and academic organisations that are in a partnership with Roche may use the Roche logo on websites or other materials. The logo use is restricted to the approved purpose only and is limited to the duration of the partnership.
> **[info]** NB: All three exceptions require the approval of the CEC-2 representative of the respective business area as well as the [Corporate Brand](mailto:corporate.branding@roche.com) team.

Please also note the [Roche Policy on Philanthropic Donations and Non-commercial Sponsorships](https://www.roche.com/dam/jcr:76b54c74-2867-42f3-909f-909bc333d5f1/en/policy_on_donations_and_non-commercial_sponsorship.pdf).
> **[note]** In the special case of architectural projects, every use of the Roche name, logo and further materials by architects and designers related to Roche buildings and premises have to be approved by the respective Roche Corporate Architect.
