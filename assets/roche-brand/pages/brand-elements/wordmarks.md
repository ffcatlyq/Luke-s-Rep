As per the Roche [logo policy](https://branding.roche.com/document/26#/logo/logo-policy) and in order to avoid diluting the Roche brand, the use of logos is reserved only for the corporate brand and for product brands.

If required, wordmarks can be used for the visual identification of departments, business units, therapeutic areas, disease areas, projects, clinical trials or initiatives. Wordmarks are to be used together with the Roche logo (they do not replace the use of the Roche logo) and are used in combination with the rest of the Roche brand elements described on this site.

Wordmarks should be created with the official [wordmark templates](https://branding.roche.com/document/29#/wordmark-generator/wordmark-templates). Wordmarks created with the official template tool can in certain cases be used externally, providing there is a good business reason (such as in the use of clinical trials).
