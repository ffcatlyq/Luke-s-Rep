---

The keyline is a graphical element inspired by the Roche logo: it derives its angle and weights from the iconic Roche hexagon shape. In a layout it is mostly used as a visual tool to separate elements to create better structure and guide the viewer.
_(video: Roche_Brand_Portal_Animation_Keyline.mp4)_

> **[info]** The keylines should only be used as directed within these guidelines. Please do not create any keylines on your own. A selection of ready-to-use templates containing the keyline are available in the [templates](https://branding.roche.com/document/29) section.


---

The keylines are built based on the three main lines of the logo. The six different keyline styles provide six different ways of conveying or structuring information. In addition, keylines serve as a visual reference to the Roche logo.

All available templates automatically ensure the correct implementation of the keylines.
