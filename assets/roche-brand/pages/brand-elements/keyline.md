Alignment + positioning

**Portrait format **

The horizontal keyline should split portrait applications on a 45/55% or 77.5/22.5% basis. These ratios are derived from the Roche proportions, as explained in the[ layout section](/r/KbF83mDbmEDqPReks-xQRtShKsZFvKUxqI0dgi0AgUQ).

**Landscape format **

The horizontal keyline should split landscape applications on a 55/45% basis or a 77.5/22.5% basis. These ratios are derived from the Roche proportions, as explained in the [layout section](/r/KbF83mDbmEDqPReks-xQRtShKsZFvKUxqI0dgi0AgUQ).

Alignment + positioning

The same split ratio applies as for the horizontal keyline:

- Portrait: 45/55% or 75.5/22.5%
- Landscape: 55/45% or 77.5/22.5%

These ratios are derived from the Roche proportions, as explained in the [layout section](/r/KbF83mDbmEDqPReks-xQRtShKsZFvKUxqI0dgi0AgUQ).
