The Roche icons feature a friendly, playful style that is consistent in terms of size, stroke and balance. Their simplicity and straightforward imagery, utilizing clear metaphors, make them readily understandable. They are grouped into three main categories: general, business, and healthcare, and evolve to suit changing usage scenarios.

These icons are intended for use in presentations and other online and offline communications, but should not be utilized as user interface elements in web or application design.

For UI icons, please refer to the [design systems](/r/48hQO3ZpCVjvFC3NyJ0ksV-CzbIkksmCSvhkeDUsbow).
Adhere to these principles when designing:
Empathetic science
The icons embody the brand's unique blend of empathy and science, conveying a sense of precision and professionalism while also exuding approachability, warmth, and honesty. They are not just a means of conveying information, but they also have the power to evoke emotions and create an impact. The Roche icons strike a balance between scientific accuracy and emotional resonance, expressing the human aspect of the brand. These are the key principles that guide their design:

Focus on simplicity
Complex and intricate icons can be difficult to comprehend, defeating the purpose of using icons to simplify information. Simple and straightforward icons are more easily understood and emotionally impactful.

Literal symbols
The use of clear and literal symbols makes icons more relatable and emotionally engaging. Aim to use symbols that are immediately recognisable, rather than abstract metaphors.

Prioritize function
The primary goal of icons is to communicate information effectively. Readability and clarity should always take precedence over aesthetics, but that doesn't mean that the icons shouldn't also be visually appealing. A successful icon will strike a balance between form and function.

To produce icons, utilise the Adobe illustrator template file provided below.
When creating new icons, always begin by reviewing the existing icons in the brand library to ensure that there's not already a fitting icon for the new concept. This step serves to minimise duplication and preserve consistency in usage, avoiding any unnecessary additions to the library.

When it comes to symbols like "delete" and "settings," follow what's already been accepted globally. There's no need to create something new. Strive for simplicity in icons. Avoid adding unnecessary details. Make sure the main message is clear and easily recognisable.

In instances where the concept is overly complex, it may not be suitable for representation through an icon.
Base grid and padding
Start your icon design on a 192x192 pixel grid with an 8 pixel inner padding for optimal alignment of elements and strokes. Keep the padding area clear of any strokes or elements.

Also consider that icons must be easily recognisable and legible at different sizes - 

192px, 96px, 48px, and 24px.
Keylines
Keyline shapes in combination with grid lines provides a structured and consistent approach to icon design. The four main shapes - circle, square, portrait rectangle, and landscape rectangle - serve as the foundation for maintaining visual proportion and consistency across the entire icon set.

To ensure adherence to these guidelines, we provide a downloadable icon grid template with keylines for use in Adobe Illustrator.
In cases where the new icon or core concept cannot be accurately represented through a single form, the circle shape should serve as the default keyline shape.
Stroke weight and clear space
Stroke weight is standardised at 8 pixels, featuring a ‘Round Cap’ style with ‘Round Joins’. Adequate clear space of 8 pixels must be maintained between lines to ensure visual clarity and avoid clutter in areas where multiple lines intersect.
Radii & the Roche angle
Icons feature two distinct rounding options - a 12 pixel radius and a less frequently used 8 pixel radius. The preferred rounding is 12 px, while the 8 px rounding is reserved for less prominent elements. Sharp corners are not utilized in the design.

Use the 49° angle inspired by the Roche logo wherever feasible, but do not feel limited to it. Ideally, the horizontal axis should align with the 49° angle as represented in the Roche logo, but applying the same to the vertical axis is also allowed.
Stay pixel-perfect
To maintain a sharp appearance, ensure that strokes and points are aligned to the grid. Incorporating numerical values that are divisible by 8 results in clear and defined icons, even at smaller sizes.
Modifiers & Line breaks
To avoid cluttering icons with too much detail,  we've created a "modifier" system. This way, a complex idea can be visualised with a basic icon and a modifier element. For example, instead of designing a lung with cancer, we use the basic lung icon of the icon set and add a tumour modifier symbol.

The modifiers are typically located in the bottom right corner, but it may be positioned elsewhere in certain circumstances. Its dimensions are about the size of 64x64 pixels.
For a clean and easy-to-understand appearance, line breaks are necessary when a modifier intersects the main lines of the icon. Breaking the lines helps to maintain both clarity and readability.

Note that there should always be a minimum of 8 pixels between two strokes (in certain situations, you can break the line in base icons for added legibility).
Colours
Icons are designed with two colours: Roche Blue and Light Blue. The primary colour is Roche Blue and Light Blue is used to highlight the icon's meaning. These are known as the Duo-Tone versions. For an icon to be added to the brand library, it must feature both the Duo-Tone and solid black variants. 

Additional colour options are generated by the system from the monochrome version – these single colours include Roche Blue, Light Blue, Accent Red, Orange, Purple, Gray and White.
Export
To meet the required specifications, an icon needs to be ungrouped prior to being exported in both duo-tone and mono-colour variations, as well as in three distinct file extensions, which include PNG, SVG (stroke version), and EPS (outlined version).

Make sure you do not leave any hidden geometry inside the .svg and eps.

The requested naming protocol for these files is "**category_icon-name_duo/mono.png/.svg/.eps**".

Icons that have been created in compliance with our brand guidelines are eligible for submission to be added to our media library. Please send the icons to [corporate.branding@roche.com](https://corporate.branding@roche.com) for consideration.
