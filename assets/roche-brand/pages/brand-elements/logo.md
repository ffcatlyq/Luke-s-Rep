---

The Roche logo size is flexible depending on the channel. To maintain clarity and legibility, the logo must not be displayed below the minimum sizes as outlined here.
Digital
**Digital (Sites & Apps):** the logo should always appear larger than 54 px wide. Minimum widths for various applications are:

- Minimum: 54 px wide
- Small: 72 px wide
- Medium: 90 px wide
- Large: 120 px wide
Print
**Print: **it should always appear larger than 12 mm wide. Minimum widths for different A-formats are:

- Minimum: 12 mm wide
- A5: 15 mm wide
- A4: 21 mm wide
- A3: 30 mm wide
> **[info]** For any questions or in the case of exceptions where these rules cannot be adhered to, please consult the Roche [Corporate Brand Team](mailto:corporate.branding@roche.com).
