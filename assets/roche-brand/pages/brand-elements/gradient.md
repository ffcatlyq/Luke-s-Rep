The Roche gradient comes in a variety of tones using the Roche colour palette.

A combination of the Roche Base and Primary tones are used.
[{"type":"heading3","children":[{"text":"Gradient examples","textStyle":"heading3"}]}]

The Gradients are created using a 49° angle that derives from the Roche hexagon. The **full gradients** use this angle and the tone runs from top left to bottom right or vice versa. 

**The split gradients** emphasize the angle even more with a diagonal line that runs bottom left to top right.
**Full gradient**

The gradient follows an angle of 49° and

slopes downwards from left to right. The colour

can run from light to dark or dark to light.

**Split gradient**

The hard edge of the Split Gradient should

slope upwards from bottom left to the top right

at an angle of 49°.
