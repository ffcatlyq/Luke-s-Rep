Co-branding is the combining of at least two distinct brands in order to strengthen the equity of both of them. This kind of combined branding is only acceptable in a few cases. The wrong application can cause large investments without return, cause confusion and convey wrong messages.

The key takeaway from the Roche co-branding guidance is to not combine two brands or their elements into a hybrid, but instead to identify which is the lead brand and then follow their corporate design to 100%.

Due to the exceptional nature of such collaborations, please consult with the Corporate Branding Team to ensure correct application.

---

Roche and Genentech co-branding
This co-branding can be explored only in exceptional circumstances and with the permission of both [Roche](mailto:corporate.branding@roche.com) and [Genentech](mailto:brand@gene.com) corporate brand teams_. _Generally:

- when the audience is international, we brand as Roche
- when it is exclusively located in the U.S., we brand as Genentech

The two logos should never be locked up together.

If you need to discuss an exceptional co-branding need, please reach out as far in advance of your deadline as possible to both corporate brand teams.
