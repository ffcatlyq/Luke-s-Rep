---

The goal of animating icons is to bring an added level of excitement and engagement to the user interface. By incorporating motion, icons become even more effective at drawing the user's attention to important information and it also adds a touch of personality and variety to the content.
Adhere to these principles when designing:

Playful touch
The design strives for a delicate balance between playfulness and sophistication, showcasing lively movements that convey both approachability and professionalism.

Opt for simplicity
In order to maintain a harmonious design, it is recommended to keep things simple, elegant and to the point. Over-animating the icons can lead to a cluttered and distracting user experience.

---

To produce animated icons, utilise the Adobe After Effects template file provided below. Importing icons in .ai format helps to preserve their vector attributes.
Composition sizes
The template file contains two compositions for animation, with the first having the same size as the static icons (192x192 pixel). All animation is performed in this composition, which is then scaled up by 3 and embedded in an 'OUTPUT' composition with additional padding (768x768 pixel). This results in the highest quality alpha channel GIF export (further details under the 'Export settings' section).
Duration and looping
The full-length animation is **5 seconds** long and operates at **25 fps**, consisting of **three stages**:

**Phase 1: Static I. **From 0s to 1s. The animation begins with a display of the icon's original appearance.

**Phase 2: Animation** From 1s to 4s. The animation transpires over a duration of 3 seconds, and consistently returns to its starting position.

**Phase 3: Static II.** From 4s to 5s. To create seamless loops, the ending state of the animation must match the initial static phase exactly.

Attention must be paid to ensuring that the starting and ending points of the icon animation match precisely, as any failure to do so can result in a noticeable glitch during playback.
Animation
To attain the intended style, it is advisable to utilise simple movements and focus on animating only fundamental attributes such as position, rotation, scale, and path. It's best to avoid any fast or sudden movements that may detract from the overall impact.
Easings
Easings are a vital component in animation, as they add a sense of fluidity and natural feel to transitions. Applying Ease In/Ease Out or Easy Ease on all keyframes ensures a smooth and desirable mood.

While there is no exact value for Keyframe Incoming and Outgoing Velocity, you should aim for calm and positive movements.
Export settings
In order to comply with the specifications, the icons must be exported with a resolution of 768x768 pixels resolution, alpha channel gif format, and with infinite loop setting. The scaled up composition from the 'OUTPUT' folder of the After Effects template file must be used for this task.   

To achieve better-quality results, we recommend using the [GifGun plugin](https://aescripts.com/gifgun/) for exporting instead of the basic After Effects alpha channel GIF setting.  

The requested naming protocol for these files is "**category_icon-name_duo_anim.gif**".
