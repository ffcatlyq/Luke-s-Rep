To ensure consistency, we urge designers to use this page as a foundation when creating illustrations.

Brand colours
The brand identity of Roche is closely tied to the use of colour. Blue is a cornerstone of the brand, serving to visually link the brand to its logo, while the use of natural Base colours adds an element of simplicity, warmth and refinement to the design. Darker tones can also be incorporated, but in moderation, and Accent colours provide a touch of brightness and flexibility in the design and highlight important information.
> **[info]** Despite not being part of the brand colour palette, green can be used (sparingly) in illustrations for depicting elements that are also green in real life, e.g. trees, plants etc. Please ensure that the chosen green hue complements the colour scheme of the illustration.

> **[info]** For easy access to Roche's Adobe CC Library, refer to the following [link.](https://assets.adobe.com/public/52a6b9df-3cd3-4af0-681e-333c1eeaeee3)

In addition to the Base and Accent colours indicated above, a supplementary skin tone palette was developed for skin tones to extend the versatility of the brand's colours, allowing for a more extensive selection and a wider range of choices to cater to diverse needs.

The use of this palette is optional. Please take note that the colours presented in this palette have been curated to serve the purpose of illustrating skin tones only, and any further application of these colours is strictly forbidden.

The ‘clear and graphic’ style is characterized by the use of imperfect geometric shapes, resulting in bold and striking visuals that convey meaning and emotion with a minimalist approach. The use of different shapes in different colours and sizes creates a sense of tension and harmony that draws the viewer's eye and adds further interest to the design.
The clear & graphic style relies on the correct application of unique lines and angles, which is characterised by the use of imperfect briefing lines, uneven curves, and non-regular angles. The goal is to achieve a well-balanced combination of these features to create illustrations that are harmonious, authentic, and possess a handcrafted look, capturing attention and effectively conveying information.

---

Visualise all content in a simple manner that doesn't compromise the message being conveyed. In essence, this means everything must be presented in its simplest form avoiding any unnecessary details. Aesthetics should not take precedence over clarity and effectiveness.

Achieving inclusivity and avoiding the reinforcement of stereotypes or biases in illustrations requires deliberate consideration of representation from diverse backgrounds. When selecting reference materials or models, be intentional in seeking out sources that feature individuals of diverse ages, genders, religions, and ethnicities, ensuring a balanced and unbiased representation.

Representation
Prioritise diversity in your illustrations by incorporating characters and individuals from various backgrounds, ethnicities, genders, ages, body types, and abilities. Ensure a truly inclusive representation by also showcasing diverse hairstyles, such as afros, braids, or hijabs, as well as clothing choices that reflect cultural traditions and customs from different regions or countries. Embrace the diversity of modern family structures by depicting a range of mixed family units, including modern mixed families, same-sex couples, and loving parents.

  This enables a reflection of the real world and provides an opportunity for people from all walks of life to see themselves represented.
Cultural sensitivity
Be mindful of cultural stereotypes and clichés in your illustrations. Recognise that people have multiple dimensions to their identities, such as race, gender, sexuality, and disability, which intersect and shape their experiences. Avoid generalisations or appropriating specific cultures. Instead, aim to celebrate and respectfully depict the diverse traditions, customs, and aesthetics of different cultures, while consulting and collaborating with individuals from those cultures for authentic and accurate representation.
Empowerment
Strive for positive and empowering portrayals of diverse individuals. Illustrate them in a way that showcases their strengths, talents, and achievements. This helps to promote inclusivity, foster empathy, and challenge biases.

---

Keep accessibility at the forefront when developing illustrations. Focus on creating visuals that are clear and easily comprehensible, taking into account factors like colour contrast and distinguishable details to ensure accessibility for individuals with different abilities.   Regularly educate yourself about accessibility guidelines and seek feedback from individuals with diverse abilities to continually enhance the accessibility of the illustrations.
