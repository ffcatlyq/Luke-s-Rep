The use of Gradients is a core component of the Roche brand identity that adds warmth and elegance to the visual system. It is created with the 49° angle of the Roche hexagon. Gradients are to be used sparingly, mostly in digital applications, and only when they enhance the overall visual experience.
_(video: Roche_Brand_Portal_Animation_Gradients.mp4)_

> **[info]** A selection of ready-to-use, tested and optimised gradients for digital and print can be found in the [gradient library](https://branding.roche.com/document/71) for download and further use. 



No additional gradients should be used or created on your own.

The Roche design system has two types of gradients:

- **Full gradients** offer complete background fills and are used in combination with the Roche keyline. Use a full gradient as a background for typography and keylines.
- **Split gradients** offer a layout device for separating typography instead of the “keylines” system.

Which gradient to use remains flexible and can be decided on a case-by-case basis. The two gradients types are never used together. Gradients should only be used when their usage enhances the design and is not just for decorative purposes only.

---

They are used as complete background fills to enhance typography and always feature the Roche keyline to create separation and better structure the content.
Full gradients can be used:

- As a background for typographic layouts
- As a background with the keyline system

---

They provide a focal point that can be used as a feature on a design application. They are used in combination with typography or on a combined layout with pictures to enhance the visual appearance.
Similar to full gradients, use split gradients when their usage enhances the design by adding warmth and elegance to it.

Split gradients can be used as a layout device to structure content as an alternative to the Roche keyline.
