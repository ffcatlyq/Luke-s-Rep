Photography is at the core of the Roche look & feel. It provides a highly visual, emotional and relatable representation of the people and processes within Roche. Pictures are authentic, engaging, elegant, uncluttered and human-centered.

Our ever-expanding [image library](/r/eKg6lZuVnmvkrBRiyNyb7jtRR9rmTHB9QW4O-BmhKDk) contains images, which can be used for all Roche materials globally.
_(video: Roche_Brand_Portal_Animation_Photography.mp4)_

The Roche image language is expressed in four core categories:

- Hero portraits
- People (individuals/groups)
- Details (hands at work)
- Abstract and macro

In addition, Roche images cover themes such as architecture or management photography.

Core traits that help to create a uniquely ‘Roche image’
Core Attributes:

- Authentic
- Honest
- Emotive
- Simple
- Engaging

Composition notes:

- Calm, simple backgrounds
- Involved in activity
- Warm tones
- No unnatural angles
- Balance of age, gender, ethnicity and seniority
People are at the core of Roche, so we portray them as heroes in our unique portrait photography style. Portraiture allows us to capture individual character and focus on the unique personalities and perspectives of our community.
> **[warning]** Please note: All our ‘Hero’ portraits are real employees at Roche. We ask you respectfully refrain from using our ‘Hero’ photos in content linked to any disease or therapeutic area that portrays them as a patient.



For patient-related communications, please refer to [this library](/r/uafkZSl9wCQgVfiXffuNRL5ves6MNGcpbzt5T_Car2A)  



Any asset published where the audience interprets it as a ‘patient’ photo or video, must by law include the caption ‘Model(s) depicted’ (modify as required) and is applicable both to print and digital touchpoints.


---

Individuals
This category covers a wide range of subjects across all aspects of the business and could include:

- Employees
- Doctors
- Patients
- Apprentices
Group
This content category covers a wide range of subjects across all aspects of our business, same as for individuals, but showing them in a joint activity with 2, 3 or more people.

---

Detail images zoom in on the action. These images always capture ‘hands at work’ in action, providing a close-up intimate moment, even within a lab or any given environment, giving a sense of working with purpose.
All images available ensure full adherence to SHE requirements.

---

Our Abstract imagery conveys atmosphere: with it we create elegant graphic imagery using macro images to show processes and objects within the Roche world.
Abstract photography is elegant, simple and graphic.

- Where possible, include Roche accent colours within an image
- Where possible, consider the Roche angle (49°) in the composition: it can run top left to bottom right or top right to bottom left
- Choose graphic elements that show the ‘beauty’ in the everyday
- Where possible, allow quiet space for text overlays
