Health is personal and this aspect is now even more reflected in our brand. With the evolved brand, Roche comes across as warm, authentic, approachable – like the company that we are.
Building the Roche brand experience is an ongoing effort. It is important that we are consistent in our communication and experience across all applications (e.g. print, digital and social, initiatives, and events).

Roche design aims to be best in class. We set design standards by adapting and applying best practices in contemporary design.

The Roche brand design is:

- minimalistic, clean and simple
- elegant and light (use of open and white space)
- warm (through human connection, use of warmer tones and reduced use of blue)
- personal, emotive and empathetic
- authentic and engaging
