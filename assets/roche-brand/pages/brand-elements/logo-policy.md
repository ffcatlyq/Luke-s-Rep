Roche follows a strict logo policy whereby the use of a logo is reserved only for the corporate brand, members of the Roche group (Genentech, Chugai, FMI, Spark, Flatiron), Pharma and Diagnostics product brands.

Other logos (such as team, franchise, project, initiative, clinical trials or department logos) are not allowed, not even for internal use. What can be used for visual identification instead is a [wordmark](/r/2fnYHc_JW8cyia8U0BSXmrq7kkYWzwhTboNLvCnbpMY).
