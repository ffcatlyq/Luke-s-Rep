Roche icons are all new. These refreshed icons are separated into three categories; general, business and healthcare. The icon set is planned to grow and diversify over time.

Our main icon set is offered as a two-tone colourway, which utilises two of the Roche blues. We also offer a second set, in a single colourway, which you can define from the Roche accent palette.

These icons can be used in your presentations and communications and are **not to be used as UI elements** in web design or application design. Please refer to our [design systems](/r/e4CRlBeHCE4HWJU4oN8tGCOX5hY4CyNnZca6xYOQla4) for UI usage.
> **[info]** You can find the ever-growing set of static icons [here](https://branding.roche.com/document/70).

The important facts
- The icons can be saved in both PNG and SVG formats.
- PNGs are suitable for digital and desktop applications, SVG is used for other touchpoints

How to colour your icons

---

To keep our brand consistent, do not re-colour the icons in any other colour than allowed, don’t change the thickness of the outlines, don’t add solid fills to sections and don’t add background shapes.
