Fully customizable templates to create your own business cards and envelopes.

To offer a convenient way to share personal contact information, QR codes can now be placed on the back of business cards. Please use a QR code generator, download the file and upload it to the second page of the business card template. 

If you are not planning to use a QR code, you can delete/discard the second page from the downloaded file._Paper stock/weight that should be used:__Letterhead (first & second page): RecyStar Nature, 80gm²Envelopes: ELCO premium, white offset, FSC, C5 = 100gm² / C4 = 120gm²Business cards & greeting cards: white Amber Graphic, FSC, 240gm²_
