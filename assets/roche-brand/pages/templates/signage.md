A separate layout is created for each room. The layouts created are to be saved on the brand page in the ‘Sigange...’ project for the relevant site, where they can then be adapted by all users activated in the project folder.

In edit mode, optional layers can be shown and hidden. To do this, select the required layer in the ‘Layers’ window on the left and then deactivate/activate it in the ‘Visibility’ window on the right.

The layout can be downloaded as a pdf and printed out. Printing is done on pre-cut paper inserts (therefore without cutting marks). The pre-cut paper inserts can be ordered from the responsible paper supplier:Paper: Olin, regular, high white matt, woodfree ECF, 150 gr./sqmCut to size: 88 x 178 mm
