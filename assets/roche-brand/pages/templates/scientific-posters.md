The scientific poster template is designed to meet scientific communication needs while staying true to our brand. It contains two sections: the header and the main content. The Roche logo and the height of the header section must not be altered.Document size:

(A0) 1189 x 841 mm in Landscape and portrait mode with templates provided in Google slides and Microsoft PowerPoint (Indesign files coming soon)

(A3) 420 x 297 mm in landscape and portrait mode with templates provided in Google slides, Microsoft PowerPoint, Microsoft Word and InDesign files.
