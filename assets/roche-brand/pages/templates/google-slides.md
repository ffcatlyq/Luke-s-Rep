Google Slides is the standard tool at Roche for the creation and sharing of presentations and slide decks. Next to easy collaboration and automatic saving in Google Drive, it offers the advantage of incorporating all the aspects of the evolved brand “out of the box”:

- standardised templates for different use cases
- new Roche font family
- colour palette
- keyline design elements
