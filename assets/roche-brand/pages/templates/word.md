In instances where Google Docs cannot be used, Microsoft Word offers an alternative to the creation of documents. The respective Word templates and add-on are automatically rolled out to all Roche computers (Windows only).

Users that do not currently have the template / add-on can manually download it via the Software Center (connection to PulseSecure required).
