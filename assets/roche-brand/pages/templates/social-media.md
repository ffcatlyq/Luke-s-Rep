Below is a library of customisable templates for you to use to create social media assets. Please note, within the templates are five different sizes that are optimised for our four main social media platforms. These are Twitter, Instagram, LinkedIn, and Facebook.

When making your assets, please consider which platforms you will be posting on and edit the templates that correspond.

Tap the eye icon of the assets you would not like to download.This will hide the asset and stop it being downloaded.

**If you’re posting an asset from a Roche Global social media account, you do not need to add a logo – it will already be shown in the accompanying profile picture.**
> **[info]** In case of feedback or questions regarding the social media templates, please reach out to [global.social_media@roche.com](mailto:global.social_media@roche.com) and cc: [corporate.branding@roche.com](mailto:corporate.branding@roche.com).

> **[warning]** The "models" in the hero portraits are employees at Roche. Do not use these photos as patient photos, e.g. when communicating about one of our disease areas.


For patient-related communications, please refer to [this library](https://branding.roche.com/r/uafkZSl9wCQgVfiXffuNRL5ves6MNGcpbzt5T_Car2A) and use stock images. Ensure you display the phrase ‘model(s) depicted’ when using stock photography.

Use one of the two templates below to create a Roche-branded post for your channel. In most cases, one of these two will be sufficient for your needs. You can edit the image, graphic, text and background colour.

If you use **stock imagery,** please ensure the phrase ‘model(s) depicted’ is displayed. You can hide this message when using images from the [Roche library](https://branding.roche.com/document/62).
