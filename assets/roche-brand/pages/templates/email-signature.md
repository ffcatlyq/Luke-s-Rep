Email signatures are text only and it is not permitted to add images such as the Roche logo to the email signature.

The concern with inserting pictures to the signatures is that various email clients may render images differently resulting in skewed or wrong sizing and depending on circumstances, the images can come up as attachments rather than as an embedded images or even errors.
> **[info]** For reference, please use this [template](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1RdDKvETIGCSAlWKI7Bxzty_dRzYq03b9GvKST-byWDQ).

**Email body text**

Google does not offer the possibility to use custom fonts in Gmail. Therefore, please write your emails using the closest font offered in Gmail: “Sans Serif Normal”.

**1. Name and title**

Use Black Sans Serif Normal for your name and function title at the end of your email. Your name should be in bold and your title should match the wording on your HR profile.You can add the way you would like to be addressed by adding the respective pronouns after your name.

**2. Company information**

The company information should be in Black Sans Serif Normal. You may add your department, building and your room number here. You can also add here the postal code, city and country where you are located.

**3. Sender information**

The sender information is set in Sans Serif Normal. The telephone and the address should remain consistent within a given site. You may include your personal network profile between the Roche e-Mail and the website.

**4. (Optional) Upcoming absences or working days **

You can inform about upcoming absences or state your working days. Include that information after the sender information (format see e-Mail text)

**5. (Optional) Call-to-action**

Every day, our e-Mails reach potential partners or talents. An e-Mail gives us the opportunity to communicate interesting facts about the company. For that, it is possible to include up to 3 links between the sender information and confidentiality notice. The links should point to Roche-owned resources, accessible for an external audience (format see e-Mail text).

**6. Confidentiality note**

The confidentiality note must be included verbatim in English at the end of your e-Mail in Sans Serif Small.

_Confidentiality Note:_

This message is intended only for the use of the named recipient(s) and may contain confidential and/or proprietary information. If you are not the intended recipient, please contact the sender and delete this message. Any unauthorised use of the information contained in this message is prohibited.

_Legal Nomenclature_

In a number of countries, the format of legal nomenclature is prescribed by law. The wording must be approved by your local legal department. The legal nomenclature is set in Sans Serif Small above the confidentiality note.

_Note:_

In replies and forwarded messages, sender and confidentiality notes are not mandatory. It is sufficient to add only your name and telephone number.

**E-mail signatures may not include any of the following additions:**

- Colours other than black
- Fonts other than Sans Serif Normal
- Italicised or underlined words
- Logos (no Roche logo or external logos)
- Promotional references, banners, slogans, or quotes
- Icons such as telephone, smiley faces, or envelope symbols
- Coloured or patterned backgrounds (wallpaper function)
- Purpose Statement
- Information about our pipeline
