In the template section, anyone can create branded assets and print-ready documents without design experience and without the need for special software.
The most important facts
- The created assets can be downloaded in the file formats PDF, JPG and PNG
- JPG and PNG are suitable for digital and desktop applications, whereas PDF is suitable for printing
- PDFs can be handled by the print service provider of your choice
