Google Docs is the standard tool at Roche for document creation and collaboration. You will find a set of templates for different business purposes in the Google Docs [template gallery](https://docs.google.com/document/u/0/?tgif=d&ftv=1):

- blank - for generic purposes
- different business letter formats
- minutes template
- memo template

The templates incorporate all aspects of the evolved brand (new font family, updated logo etc.) and are available in DIN A4 and US Letter formats.
